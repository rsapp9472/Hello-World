### 1. Saudi plan to retake Bab el-Mandeb — SURFACE
Score: 9. new_fact (not in 8am, noon, or flash list).

Reuters, datelined Riyadh Oct 2 and marked “hasn’t been previously reported,” says Saudi Arabia is preparing a Yemeni-led offensive, with Saudi air cover, to reverse last month’s Houthi seizure of Bab el-Mandeb. Two options: a narrow coastal push, or that plus synchronized attacks in Al-Bayda, Marib, Taiz, and Al-Jawf. More than 100,000 Yemeni troops could be mobilized. Launch estimates run from within a week to after the US midterms. US role described as intelligence, advisers, and targeting, not combat; Trump had already refused direct strikes. Pakistan is described as defensive support inside the kingdom (on the order of 30,000–40,000 troops), not a Yemen ground force. Allies have not confirmed; Riyadh, the Yemeni government, the Houthis, and US military did not comment on the record. This is a new chokepoint fact on top of the noon stockpile release: a ground fight at the Red Sea gate that is substituting for a closed Hormuz, with retaliation risk to Saudi oil.
