## Track A — World / US

**Russia and Germany hold first foreign-minister meeting since 2022.** Sergei Lavrov and Johann Wadephul met Saturday on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly — the first such talks since before the full-scale invasion of Ukraine. Wadephul pressed for a Black Sea grain-export deal and a ceasefire; Lavrov said he heard nothing Berlin had not already said in public, and said Russia is discussing safe-navigation ideas with India, Egypt, and Turkey.  
https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/un-russia-germanys-top-diplomats-hold-first-talks-since-2022-2026-09-26/  
(Reuters, 5:19 p.m. UTC / 1:19 p.m. ET.)

**Trump says he approved much weaker vehicle fuel-economy rules; DOT finalizes Monday.** Officials said Saturday the Transportation Department will on Monday finalize sharply lower fuel-economy standards through 2031, undoing the Biden push toward higher EV production. Trump said he had approved the standards; Secretary Sean Duffy called it “a major victory for America’s auto workers” coming Monday. The shape matches the administration’s December proposal (fleetwide 34.5 mpg by 2031 versus 50.4 mpg under Biden). DOT’s own estimates on that proposal: about $930 off the average new-vehicle price, and roughly 100 billion more gallons of fuel burned through 2050. The move lands while U.S. drivers are paying sharply higher fuel prices since the war with Iran began in late February.  
https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-says-he-approved-fuel-economy-standards-ending-biden-ev-mandate-2026-09-26/  
(Reuters, 7:26 p.m. UTC / 3:26 p.m. ET.)

**Boeing 737 MAX landing-navigation software glitch becomes public.** The Wall Street Journal reported Saturday, citing company documents, that a previously undisclosed cockpit-software update can knock out automated flight guidance on a landing after a missed approach when crews change the planned path. Boeing told Reuters it informed all 737 operators last month, pointed them at existing procedures, and is writing a permanent software fix. The FAA said it will convene a Corrective Action Review Board and act if it finds a safety concern. Southwest and United have asked Boeing not to deliver new MAX jets with the affected software, per the Journal; how many airplanes are already flying it was not clear. This is not a grounding.

**Hurricane Nolo — update only (noon item was Category 2 south of the Big Island).** At the window close, Nolo was a Category 1 hurricane with 90 mph winds, moving west at 5 mph, center about 160 miles south-southwest of South Point. The hurricane watch is no longer up. A tropical storm warning remains for Hawaii County and a tropical storm watch for Maui County. The center is forecast to stay well south of the islands and to become a major hurricane on Sunday over open water. Life-threatening flood and mudslide risk is unchanged on the eastern and southern Big Island (storm totals 10–20 inches, isolated 25). Significant storm-surge threat has diminished; dangerous surf continues. The drop from Category 2 and the watch change occurred after the 5 a.m. HST advisory (still 105 mph and a hurricane watch) and before this 2 p.m. HST bulletin.
