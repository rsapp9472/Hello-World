**1. Hurricane Nolo stalled south of the Big Island; tropical-storm conditions underway.** importance 8. new_fact=yes.
NHC Advisory 24 (5:00 a.m. HST / 11:00 a.m. ET; bulletin 14:56 UTC) puts Category 2 Nolo nearly stationary about 145 miles south of South Point, winds 105 mph. Tropical-storm conditions are occurring in Hawaii County, with hurricane-force gusts possible in steep terrain; a hurricane watch and tropical-storm warning remain up, and a tropical-storm watch covers Maui County. NHC expects a westward turn later today, the center staying south of the islands, and strengthening to a major hurricane tonight or early Sunday. The stall itself started overnight; this advisory is the in-window confirmation that it has not moved off and impacts are already on land. Ground there is still wet from Hurricane Lala in August.
