## Flash check — G-7 diesel + crude (up to 100mn bbl)

**Confirmed in this window, and it stepped up past a bare “agreed Friday” flag.**

The official leaders’ text published today: a coordinated IEA release of **100 million barrels beginning immediately over four months**, with a **front-loaded substantial diesel release in the first 20 days**; G7 refinery-maintenance coordination; an IEA implementation report before 20 days; and a pledge to **refrain from energy-export restrictions among G7 countries**. Trump’s Truth Social post (Europe releasing a “massive amount” of diesel, “begin immediately”) hit Reuters at **14:08 UTC**. NBC’s write-through is **10:22 AM EDT, updated 11:21 AM EDT**, including von der Leyen welcoming no export bans on allies. Politico at **16:16 CET (14:16 UTC)** adds the step-up that matters: France reads the export pledge as the **U.S. diesel-export ban coming off the table**; Macron said Trump was “very clear” there would be no ban. Same piece carries the caveat — two diplomats called it a **restatement of the March IEA 400mn-barrel commitment**, not obviously new barrels.
