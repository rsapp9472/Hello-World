Additional rain 3–6 inches, local 10, from the southern Appalachians (northeast Georgia into the western Carolinas) and from the upper South Carolina coast into eastern North Carolina; 1–2 inches more across the rest of the Southeast into north Florida. Considerable flash, urban, and river flooding, including landslides, possible in the southern Appalachians. Significant river flooding on the western Florida Panhandle crests today through Sunday.
